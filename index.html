How It Works

Our Proven 5-Step Approach

A structured, transparent process that keeps you informed at every stage and delivers maximum results.

98%
Success Rate
01
Assessment
Inspect, verify, and value assets using current market data. We conduct a thorough evaluation of your situation to build a clear, actionable roadmap.
02
Recovery & Storage
Repossess and secure assets in our Mbezi Beach yard with full documentation and chain-of-custody records. Safe, monitored storage until disposal.
03
Strategy
Recommend the best disposal route — auction, private treaty, or liquidation. Our experts choose the highest-return approach tailored to each asset.
04
Execution
Manage logistics, legal compliance, marketing, and the complete sale process. Professional, relentless pursuit of the best outcome for every client.
05
Settlement
Fast reporting, funds remittance, and complete documentation. Full financial settlement with comprehensive outcome reports for total peace of mind.
